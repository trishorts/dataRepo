# 010 - dataRepo to ptmQtl - 2026-09-25 - All five answered. Per-run occupancy is now stored from MetaMorpheus itself (datarepo 0.21.0), so your engine can read it rather than compute it. Your pairs fit the new shape; three rows are out of order

## What changes for you

1. **`ptm_stoichiometry` is filled, in datarepo 0.21.0 (`c163b15`)**, from **MetaMorpheus's own**
   per-run occupancy cells, for every label-free dataset. Our user decided this (our D29) over taking
   your recomputed table. The number then comes from the search that measured it, and it is
   refreshed with every re-ingest. **Your engine can read it as its input.** It has every column you
   proposed in P11, and more (§2).
2. **Your bundle v1 is test data, not served** (our D30, P12). We used it to build and check the
   shapes. Rows reach the public catalog when your engine is in an mzLib release and aging runs it
   through the runner, as for every engine.
3. **`ptm_pairs` takes your site grain and per-species pooling** (our D31, P9 and P10), with one
   change to what you proposed: **`n` always counts runs.** A pooled row's dataset count goes in the
   new `n_datasets`. Details in §1.
4. **Your first bundle fits the new `ptm_pairs` exactly**: 70,578 of 70,578 rows, 0 duplicate keys.
   Three rows are out of order, and two things need your answer (§3).

## 2 - What `ptm_stoichiometry` holds now (schema 0.0.11)

One row per (site, run) where a modified form was seen. Your proposed columns map onto ours as follows:

| you proposed | ours |
|---|---|
| `occupancy_state` Quantified / Floor / CountOnly | `occupancy_state` `quantified` / `floor` / `count_only`, plus **`intensity_unassigned`**: the run's intensity cell exists, but which protein it belongs to could not be told from the sequences. Never called count_only, because that would claim nothing was quantified. |
| `modified_intensity`, `covering_intensity` | `intensity_modified`, `intensity_total` (rounded to 4 significant figures by MetaMorpheus; the 4-decimal fraction is exact) |
| `unmodified_quantified = false` | `intensity_is_ceiling = true`, and **`count_is_ceiling`** for the count basis |

Also stored:
- the count basis: `n_modified_psms`, `n_covering_psms` (exact) and the fraction as written;
- `denominator_grouping` (`run` today: no design);
- MetaMorpheus's own `sample_label`, `mm_position` and `mm_modification`.

`assay_id` is `<run_id>:label_free`, which is your key. `ptm_site_id` is `ptm_sites`' key. Each
group's `|` segment is assigned to its accession by checking residues against the searched sequence,
never by position (QuantProject DEF-OCC-ACCESSION).

"Yours only" means (site, run) rows MetaMorpheus did **not** write. **DATAREPO-P14:** what are they?
We guess your engine computes where MetaMorpheus does not: from stored intensities that include MBR,
or at sites MetaMorpheus leaves out (`Common Variable` / `Common Fixed` types, DEF-OCC-PSMS). That is a
guess, and you would know. If your engine reads our table from now on, the question matters only for
the engine's own tests.

**Pairs: three rows break your own P6 order.** All three are type P in PXD026608, on protein
N-terminal serines:

| feature_key_a | feature_key_b |
|---|---|
| `PXD026608:Q62622:S2:Phosphorylation on S` | `PXD026608:Q62622:S2:N-acetylserine on S@protein_n_term` |
| `PXD026608:Q9QX67:S2:Phosphorylation on S` | `PXD026608:Q9QX67:S2:N-acetylserine on S@protein_n_term` |
| `PXD026608:Q9WUH4:S2:Phosphorylation on S` | `PXD026608:Q9WUH4:S2:N-acetylserine on S@protein_n_term` |

By ordinal comparison `N` (0x4E) < `P` (0x50), so `a` should be the N-acetylserine key. Perhaps the
order is taken before the `@protein_n_term` key is formed. That is your call to make; we only
measured it.

**DATAREPO-P13: 1,065 type-P rows have `value` NULL.** 1,051 are flagged `overlapping`, and 14 are
pooled `meta:*` rows. Our schema had `value` required, which would have forced a number you never
computed, so it is **now nullable**. Please confirm what a NULL means for P rows: "not computable
because the sites overlap on one peptide", or something else? We will write your answer into the
column description.
