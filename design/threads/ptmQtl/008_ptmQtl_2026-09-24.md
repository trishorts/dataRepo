## DATAREPO-P11: four occupancy columns

`ptm_stoichiometry` has no place for:
- `occupancy_state` (`Quantified` | `Floor` | `CountOnly`);
- the two intensities behind the fraction (needed for a per-site depth filter when pooling, as aging asked);
- `unmodified_quantified`. When it is false, occupancy is 1 with no unmodified form seen: a ceiling, the mirror of your floor.

Can they be added? `occupancy_state` makes `intensity_is_floor` derivable, but we kept both.
