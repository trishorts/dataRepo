### The 264 · a hypothesis, not a finding

Target accessions, position > 0, in occupancy and not in `ptm_sites`. Led by:

```
Deamidation on N  60    Ammonia loss on N  22    Methylation on K  14
Deamidation on Q  41    Fe[III] on D       22    Citrullination on R 12
Carbamyl on K     26    Hydroxylation on P 16    Fe[III] on E / Sodium on D  8 each
```

**Our hypothesis, straight from the two definitions rather than from the data:** `DEF-OCC-PSMS`
counts an ambiguous PSM in the denominator of *every position it covers*, while `ptm_site_rows`
writes a site only where the modification's position is **determinate**. So a site covered only by
PSMs that never determinately placed the modification would have an occupancy denominator and no site
row — correctly, on both sides.

If that is right, **the 264 are not a defect in either component**; they are the boundary between a
population and a placement, and R7 should skip them rather than either of us "fixing" it. We have not
tested it, we are not asking you to, and we would rather record the hypothesis with its name on it
than let the number sit unexplained. We will test it when we build R7 and tell you either way.

**It is 2.1 % of occupancy sites**, so it does not gate anything.

If you implement `ptm_stoichiometry` in the ingester — and we think that is where it belongs, since

```
non-empty occupancy cells          918              7,307             4,413

```

Parsed from the bundles' own inputs at catalog `817c2a1def53433f`.

**Three parsing traps, because they will cost you a day each.** (1) **The modification name contains
commas** — `pos52[N6,N6,N6-trimethyllysine on K,info:fraction=1.00(1/1)]` — so splitting the cell on
`,` silently mis-parses a real modification in real data. (2) The pair is authoritative in
`CountOccupancy_` and the fraction in `IntensityOccupancy_`, not both in both. (3) A cell over ~32,000
characters is replaced wholesale by the literal `Output too long for Excel`, and the file does not
record whether `WriteExcelCompatibleTSVs` was on — we measured 0 in 12,638 cells, so it is latent, not
live.

We have asked pyMzLib for a typed reader (REQ-PYMZ-3, our 008 to them) so that neither of us is
maintaining a regex for a format we do not own. If that lands before you build, take it.
