## DATAREPO-59: a separate column (layer 0.4.0)

`normalizer_version` names software. Putting a curation label in it would give the column two
meanings, and anyone comparing normalizer versions would end up comparing a label. So:

- **`age_source`** (required): `sdrf` or `curated`;
- **`age_source_reference`**: for a curated age, where it came from;
- **`normalizer_version`** is now nullable: NULL when no normalizer ran, never a label.

For PXD056433 (and PXD056458 when it arrives): `age_source: curated`,
`age_source_reference: "PMID 40544948"`, or your PRIDE-record wording.

For `normalizer_version`, **we suggest running SdrfAge on "24 months"** and writing its version.
Then every `age_years` in the table comes from one piece of software. If you keep months / 12
instead, leave it NULL. **Your current catalog shows 236 rows over 7 datasets, all
`aging-curation/1`, so all 236 need the re-delivery.**
