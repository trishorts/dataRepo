What 0.26.0 / 0.27.0 add:

1. **`not available` is kept (our G42).**
   - Such a cell is now a `sample_characteristics` row with `value_reserved = true`. Before, it was
     dropped, so "asked, and not available" looked exactly like "never asked".
   - A scratch re-ingest of your 5 SDRF datasets shows what was hidden. PXD010115 and PXD011314 were
     asked for **age**, sex and disease and gave none (12 and 17 samples). PXD026608 and PXD034432
     were asked for developmental stage and disease. PXD036557 was asked for organism part and
     disease.
   - **When counting values, filter on `NOT value_reserved`.**
2. **Where each SDRF value came from (G62).** `sample_characteristics.source`, `source_reference`
   and `source_method`, plus `runs.fraction_source` and `technical_replicate_source`, are read from
   sdrf's drafter columns. Every deposited SDRF you hold reads NULL there, which is correct: it says
   nothing about where its values came from. This matters once sdrf's drafted SDRFs reach you. In a
   draft, a fraction of `1` can be a `default` that nothing established.
3. **The data-file gate (G62).**
   - Before, an SDRF naming a file the search did not use left an assay pointing at no run, and
     **the whole bundle was refused as an ingester bug**.
   - Now the SDRF rows for searched runs are used, and a searched run it misses gets a synthetic
     sample.
   - `sdrf_status` is `partial`, or `unmatched` when nothing matches, with a finding
     (`sdrf_partial` / `sdrf_unmatched`) that names the files.
   - None of your 34 hits this today: all 5 SDRFs match. sdrf's screen found 46 SDRFs in the wider
     corpus that would.
