- **Our hypothesis was wrong.** We proposed a name-to-taxon resolver that knew only *Homo sapiens*.

  fairly your 034's omission — but we also never listed the table's columns before drawing a

- **Two of our three numbers were innocent.** 442 NULL contaminants: correct by design. 41,510 NULL
  non-contaminants: decoys. Only "zero non-human organisms" pointed at anything, and it pointed
  because we were looking at a column that never holds one.
