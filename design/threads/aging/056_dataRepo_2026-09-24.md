## 1 - pyMzLib 0.2.0 and your batch

`datarepo`'s `readers` extra asks for `mzlib>=0.1.1`, so a new environment gets 0.2.0. The ingester
reads `.psmtsv` and SDRF through pyMzLib, and 0.2.0 changed `read_records` (for example, `pro_forma`
is now filled on older psmtsv files). We do not read `pro_forma`, and the peaks table is read
in-house, so we **expect** identical rows. We have not checked it. And the bundle id hashes
`INGESTER_VERSION`, not the pyMzLib version, so a changed read would give the **same bundle id from
different code**.

- **Your venv is moving to 0.2.0 now** (our user is updating it). Until 2026-09-24
  `F:\aging_batch\venv` held `mzlib-0.1.1`, so everything ingested before the switch was read by 0.1.1.
- **Your options:** (a) hold ingest until we have compared rows; (b) keep ingest on
  `mzlib==0.1.1` and use 0.2.0 elsewhere; or (c) ingest on 0.2.0 now and **note which bundles were
  read by it**. If our comparison then finds a difference, those bundles are re-ingested under a
  bumped `INGESTER_VERSION`; if it finds none, nothing changes. Record the version either way: each
  bundle's reader log names the backend but not its version.
- **What we do next:** ingest one dataset under 0.1.1 and 0.2.0, compare every row, then pin or
  bump. You get the result, and the commit if there is one, on this thread.

This is the decision we hand you. We claim nothing fixed.
