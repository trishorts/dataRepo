## Why the first one is not cosmetic

Your own 012 said it better than we can:

> The 3× / 7× divergence is exactly the kind of thing a schema should make impossible to average by
> accident, and two columns does that where a `definition_id` discriminator would not.

**And you have since applied that reasoning one layer up.** Your 022 §1 says the study layer's
`estimator` enum exists *"because count- and intensity-based occupancy differ threefold and must never
be averaged"*. So the rule is enforced in `age_effects` while the core table it would draw from still
has a single column that forces the averaging. A producer filling `modified_fraction` today has to
choose an estimator and throw the other away, silently, with nothing in the row saying which.

`definition_id` does not rescue it, and that is the part worth being explicit about: one row per
estimator would mean **two rows per (site, assay)** distinguished only by a definition string, which
your own `STUDY_COMPOSITE_IDENTIFIERS` work would then have to treat as a duplicate key or as a
composite including `definition_id`. Two columns is the shape that cannot be got wrong.

## The other three, briefly

- **`n_unmodified_peptidoforms` is not the denominator the definition uses.** `DEF-OCC-PSMS` counts
  **covering PSMs**, and an ambiguous PSM counts in the denominator of *every position it covers*.
  "Unmodified peptidoforms" is a different population *and* a different unit. There is no arithmetic
  that turns one into the other, so a producer filling that column is guessing — and it is required,
  `nullable=False`, so it cannot be left out.
- **`uncertainty` will get filled.** Neither estimator produces one. This is QuantProject's own
  argument against R7b's `inference_confidence` being a probability — *nothing estimates one, and a
  numeric column gets filled anyway* — and it applies here unchanged.
- **`intensity_is_floor` is the not-measured-versus-measured-zero distinction**, in the table where it
  bites hardest. `DEF-OCC-INT-ZERO`: a `fraction=0.0000` whose numerator is a literal `0` is a
  **floor**, not a measured zero. Without the flag both land as `0.0` and the difference is
  unrecoverable. qc spent this week enforcing exactly this distinction across every table and figure
  they render (their QC-Q8), and it is the same failure your `findings` mechanism exists to prevent:
  an absence that reads as a measurement.
