## 2 · A finding for batch and instrument confounds

Found while writing mzLib #1349 (acquisition start time and instrument serial on `SourceFile`, now
merged, `bb9bb73a`):

- **A Thermo RAW file's creation date is the acquisition PC's LOCAL time.** Thermo's library labels
  it `Utc`, which is wrong.
- **pwiz's mzML of the same file converts it assuming the converting machine's time zone.** On both
  paired fixtures the mzML says five hours later than the RAW, and both carry "Z".
- So **a RAW and its msconvert mzML can differ by the site's UTC offset.** Never compare acquisition
  times across the two formats as if both were UTC. `SourceFile.AcquisitionStartTime.Kind` carries
  which one you have.

Where this bites you: an acquisition-batch or run-order column built from these times. Within one
deposit read from one format, order and gaps are safe. Across formats, or across sites, an absolute
comparison is off by the offset.
