## 2 · DATAREPO-17 · which PSM population `DEF-OCC-PSMS` counts over

### Our §4.2 diagnosis was wrong, and we reproduced yours

We re-ran it against the catalog and get the same shape you do: of the 73 target-group accessions
carrying a site we could not find in `ptm_sites`, accepted PSMs break down **2D 8,077 · level-1 1,995 ·
3 386 · 4 101 · 5 92 · 2A 5 · 2B 4**, and **20 of the 73 have no accepted level-1 PSM at all** — your
"20 appear only above level 1", independently. 2D dominating is the whole story, and our
ambiguous-protein-group explanation was a guess we should have tested before sending. Thank you for
checking it instead of building on it.

### The answer, in two parts

**(a) The q-value cut and the ambiguity levels.** `DEF-OCC-PSMS` is QuantProject's definition, and its
v3 text (their 016) is explicit on both:

> **PSMs:** the protein group's PSMs that pass the task's q-value threshold (default 0.01) **at the PSM
> level** (`ProteinScoringAndFdrEngine.cs:62`), within the group's files.
> **Ambiguous PSMs:** they count in the denominator of every position they cover, and mark no site.

So: **q ≤ 0.01 at PSM level, and no ambiguity-level filter of any kind.** There is no level-1
restriction in the occupancy population. That is why 83% of your missing sites are covered only at
level ≥ 2 — those PSMs are in the population, and MetaMorpheus's own "ambiguous" notion (which
suppresses *marking a site*) is not the same thing as the 1/2A/2D/3/4/5 ambiguity level.

**Your proposed default is the right one**, and we would take it: emit a site from any accepted PSM
whose modification position is determinate, whatever the ambiguity level, plus a column recording the
best (lowest) level that placed it, so level-1-only remains one `WHERE` away. It is additive and it
reproduces today's table exactly.

One caveat we want on the record rather than buried: **the ambiguity-level point is our reading of
QuantProject's definition, not their ruling.** We are asking them to confirm it, and will tell you
either way. Do not freeze the relaxation on our word alone if it is expensive to change.

**(b) The 36 with no accepted PSM at all are contaminants.** This one we can settle with data.

Occupancy is computed on the protein **group**, and `aging:DEF-PROTEINGROUP-1PCT v1` **includes
contaminant groups** (our D20 — the protein-group headline uses `!IsDecoy`, unlike the PSM and peptide
lines). Your `ptm_sites` derivation filters `target_decoy = 'target'`, which drops **1,508 contaminant
PSMs** in this dataset. So a contaminant group has occupancy and can have no "accepted" PSM covering it.

Of our 221 missing sites, **19 sit on contaminant-only groups** — all of them on `P02769` (bovine serum
albumin) and `P00761` (porcine trypsin).

**The worked example you asked for — `P02769`, position 304:**

```sql
SELECT target_decoy, ambiguity_level, count(*) FROM psms
WHERE list_contains(protein_accessions,'P02769') GROUP BY 1,2;
  contaminant  1     589
  contaminant  2D     81
  contaminant  3      27
  decoy        3       6

SELECT count(*) FROM ptm_sites WHERE protein_accession IN ('P02769','P00761');
  0
```

589 contaminant PSMs at **ambiguity level 1**, q = 0.000369 — about as unambiguous and as well below
threshold as a PSM gets — and zero `ptm_sites` rows. Nothing about level explains it; it is purely the
target filter. A representative row:

```
PXD036557:QE-002129_GM8_c:28159:1   contaminant   level 1   q=0.000369
SHC[UNIMOD:4]IAEVEKDAIPENLPPLTADFAEDKDVC[UNIMOD:4]…
```

**What we would like you to do with that is a judgement call we do not want to make alone.** Contaminant
sites are real measurements of real molecules, and BSA and trypsin carbamidomethyl sites are genuinely
useful as a process control — we used the contaminant share as a QC metric (our S17, 7.0% of protein
intensity). But they are not the sample. Our preference: **keep them, and carry `target_decoy` on
`ptm_site`** so they can be excluded by default and inspected on purpose — the same shape as your
`excluded_fixed` / `excluded_common_variable` proposal in §3, and for the same reason. Say if you would
rather they simply stay out, and we will make the benchmark expect that instead.

### On your latent span bug

### 4.3 · Two columns that exist and are never populated

- **`ptm_sites.localization_score`: 0 of 1,370.** This kills E5 ("is this phosphosite localized
  confidently, or is it ambiguous?"), which we had classified as answerable until we ran it. For
  PTM work the difference between a localized and an ambiguous site is the difference between a
  claim and a guess. MetaMorpheus does not write a localization probability for ordinary GPTMD
  hits, so this may be a column that should be nullable-by-design with that stated — but then the
  answer to E5 needs to be a refusal, not an empty cell.
- **`runs.acquisition_datetime`: 0 of 18.** This is our G25 / REQ-PYMZ-2 and it is a pyMzLib reader
  gap, not yours. Worth noting that **`instrument_model` *is* populated** ("Q Exactive Plus"), so
  half of G25 has quietly landed and we had not noticed.
