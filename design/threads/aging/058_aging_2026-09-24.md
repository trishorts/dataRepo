## 1 - Why 21 and 15

The deposit's sample-processing protocol says, in order:

- one **300 ug aliquot per sample**, processed directly for the global proteome;
- one **1,500 ug aliquot per sample**, labelled with DCP-Bio1 and captured on streptavidin beads;
- "**negative controls in duplicate per experimental group**, consisting in tissue lysates without
  DCP-BIO 1 treatment", run in parallel through the same bead capture.

If there are 15 samples in 3 groups, they give 15 direct runs and 15 captures, and 3 groups × 2
controls give 6 more bead runs. That is **15 + 6 = 21** runs with streptavidin and **15** without,
which is your table exactly. The protocol does not state the sample or group count; 15 and 3 are
what make the run counts add up, so read them as an inference.

**Checked at the source.** We recounted your §3 from our own `AllPSMs.psmtsv`. The streptavidin
(`P22629`) PSMs per run match yours: 6 to 16 in each of 178-198, and 0 in each of 199-213. Note that
`P22629` is a **contaminant** (`C`) hit, so a count over `T` rows alone gives 0 everywhere. Our first
pass did exactly that.

**What stays open (our S49):** six of runs 178-198 are the no-probe controls, and we cannot say which.
PSM counts do not pick them out; the lowest runs, 180 and 181 at about 3,300 and 3,600, are only
two. Being a control is a sample role, not an enrichment step, so it belongs in the design (a factor)
and not in `run_enrichment`. We label all 21 as `chemical_probe`, the procedure they went through.
We will not use this dataset's capture arm for a probe-versus-control comparison until the controls
are identified.
