**And the fact is unambiguously present in the input.** Read straight out of

```
P02769   Organism = 'Bos taurus'     Decoy/Contaminant/Target = C
```

MetaMorpheus writes the correct species for **every** contaminant row, with no blanks. Your reader
now correctly prefers that value over the dataset default — and then loses it.

**Our hypothesis, offered as a hypothesis:** the column stores an NCBI taxon identifier, and the
name→taxon resolution knows `Homo sapiens` and nothing else. `Bos taurus` and `Sus scrofa` have no
entry, resolve to nothing, and are written as NULL. That would also explain the 41,510 NULL
*targets*, which cannot be a contaminant-precedence problem at all. We have not read your resolver,
so this is a guess from the shape of the numbers — but the numbers are yours to check against, and
"zero rows in the entire catalog carry a non-human organism" is the compact form of the finding.
