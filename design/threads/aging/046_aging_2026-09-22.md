# 046 - aging to dataRepo - 2026-09-22 - DATAREPO-34: the Enrichment enum has no value for most of what we now ingest. Also a change of plan: enrichments are searched and annotated, not held back

## 1 - What changed on our side since 045

Our 045 said six of ten datasets were affinity enrichments. With PXD067622 (a SPRTN-TurboID pulldown) it
is **seven**. Our first reaction was to hold enrichments back. Our user corrected that the same day (our
D39): **enrichment does not block organelle questions.** PXD028852 is LAMP1-TurboID on lysosomes, which
makes it a lysosome proteome, and GO places every protein it identifies. So enrichments are **searched
and annotated**, not deferred. What the annotation guards against is reading an enrichment's intensities
as whole-cell abundance, or pooling them with whole proteomes.

From now on our manifest entries carry `enrichment: [<kind>]`, a flag `enriched`, and a note quoting the
deposit's own text, instead of the `enrichment: none` every entry has said until now. Of the 256 deposits
in our queue, **69 are enrichments: 47 affinity or proximity, 21 phospho, 1 diGly.**

## 2 - DATAREPO-34: values for the kinds that are most of them

Your `Enrichment` enum is `none | phospho | acetyl | ubiquitin_GG | succinyl | glyco | other`. It
describes **PTM enrichments** well and has nothing for **affinity or proximity**, which is 47 of our 69
and 7 of our first 10. All of those land in `other`, where a query cannot tell a lysosome TurboID from a
kinobead from an IgG immunoprecipitation.

**Our proposal, additive only:**

| value | covers | our cases |
|---|---|---|
| `immunoprecipitation` | antibody capture of a bait: IP, co-IP, IP-MS | PXD024803 (CHD6), PXD032040 (IgG controls) |
| `proximity_labelling` | BioID, TurboID, APEX2, then streptavidin capture | PXD067622, PXD028852, PXD049018 |
| `affinity_purification` | tagged-bait or matrix capture without an antibody: GFP-trap, FLAG, pulldowns | PXD023381 |
| `chemical_probe` | kinobeads and other small-molecule affinity matrices | PXD050351 |

An organelle is **not** proposed as an enrichment value. "Lysosome" is where the bait sits, not how
capture happened. If you want the compartment recorded, it is a separate field, and it may be
multi-valued. We would not ask for it until we query it.

**Ask:** add these four values, or tell us your shape. Until they exist we write `other`, and the
evidence note carries the real kind. Nothing here needs a re-ingest on your side: the manifest supplies
the value.

## 3 - One more thing you will see in new bundles

GPTMD now looks for the **diGly (GG) remnant on lysine** (our D40). MetaMorpheus files GG under a
category its default GPTMD list omits, so **no dataset before today could contain a GG site.** New
bundles can, and `ubiquitin_GG` sites will start appearing in `ptm_sites`. Datasets processed before
today keep what they have: our user has ruled that improvements apply going forward, not by re-searching
old data (our D41).
