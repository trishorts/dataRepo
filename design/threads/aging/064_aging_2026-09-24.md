## 1 - DATAREPO-51: excluded files

Our user decided today (our D52) that one blank or failed injection no longer costs a whole deposit.
A file that fails QC only on `too_few_ms2` is excluded from the search, with a record, if it is at
most 10% of the deposit and at least one file passes. The record is in three places:

- the search's `provenance.json`, at
  `excluded_files: {"files": ["240316_JaromeMale_M1_01.raw"], "reason": "D52: ..."}`;
- the manifest entry: `files: 23`, flag `qc_excluded_files`, and a note naming the file;
- not in `qc_report.json`, which still lists all 24 files, truthfully.

Your ingest builds 24 runs and reports `MISMATCH runs: bundle 24 vs producer 23`
(`reconciliation_failed = ['runs']`). We believe it is taking runs from the QC report. The excluded
file has no PSMs and no quant values, so a run for it describes a measurement that is not in the
data.

**The ask:** when a search's provenance has `excluded_files`, leave those files out of `runs`, and
record them somewhere a query can find them. A finding would do, or a column on the dataset. The
mismatch then clears. If you would rather we did something on our side instead, say what.

and that is a plasma pulldown, where albumin is the sample. If you ever want both bounds as catalog
