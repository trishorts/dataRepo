## 3 · Three corrections, and one of them is ours

### 3.1 · `low_id_rate` reports the wrong PSM count — our fault

The finding reads `27958/266402 = 10.5% of MS2 identified`, and the `id_rate` metric is `0.1049`.
That numerator is `aging:DEF-PSM-FDRENGINE`, not the canonical `aging:DEF-PSM-1PCT`. The right
figure is **`26582/266402 = 9.98%`**.

This is our number — it comes out of our `provenance.json`, and it is the same class of error as
the protein-group counts we retracted in QuantProject 018 (our S23). **We will supply corrected
provenance, so please do not patch it on your side.** It would be good to have it right before the
v0.1 freeze, since it is a headline quality number that will be read by people who will not check it.

### 3.2 · Eight `PROVISIONAL:` definition IDs survive in the bundle

The bundle was written nine minutes before our 008 issued the real ones, so this is timing, not
disagreement. `MS2-COUNT`, `PEPTIDE-COUNT-1PCT`, `PRECURSOR-COUNT`, `PROTEIN-GROUP-COUNT-1PCT` and
`RUN-MINUTES` are `aging:DEF-*` now (D20). `PROTEIN-INTENSITY` and `PROTEIN-SPECTRAL-COUNT` are
**correctly still provisional** — QuantProject has not ruled on them, and we would rather they stay
provisional than be given an ID that implies they have.

### 3.3 · Two columns that exist and are never populated

- **`ptm_sites.localization_score`: 0 of 1,370.** This kills E5 ("is this phosphosite localized
  confidently, or is it ambiguous?"), which we had classified as answerable until we ran it. For
  PTM work the difference between a localized and an ambiguous site is the difference between a
  claim and a guess. MetaMorpheus does not write a localization probability for ordinary GPTMD
  hits, so this may be a column that should be nullable-by-design with that stated — but then the
  answer to E5 needs to be a refusal, not an empty cell.
- **`runs.acquisition_datetime`: 0 of 18.** This is our G25 / REQ-PYMZ-2 and it is a pyMzLib reader
  gap, not yours. Worth noting that **`instrument_model` *is* populated** ("Q Exactive Plus"), so
  half of G25 has quietly landed and we had not noticed.

## 4 · One gap that is entirely ours

**G8, "what is the contaminant share per run?", fails because there is no contamination metric in

`DEF-CONTAM-PSM`, and then never emitted it as a metric. It will appear in the corrected
provenance alongside 3.1.

## 6 · Incidentally: the first real scientific answer out of the repository

O1 asks whether progerin is detected by its junction peptide in PXD036557. The catalog answers it:

