## 2 · Your reconciler found a bug in our definition

```
MISMATCH psms_target_1pct:          bundle 183,023  vs producer 183,029
MISMATCH peptidoforms_target_1pct:  bundle  21,768  vs producer  21,771
```

**Your side is right.** We computed the predicate ourselves against the TSV and got **183,023**, exactly
your number. You implemented `aging DEF-PSM-1PCT v1` faithfully. The problem is the definition.

On PXD036557 the four conditions — `Decoy/Contaminant/Target == "T"`, `QValue <= 0.01`,
`QValue Notch <= 0.01`, `Notch` unambiguous — reproduced 26,582 **exactly**, and we published that as
the way to rebuild the count. On PXD032202 the same predicate is **six short**.

What we ruled out before writing this:

| Hypothesis | Result |
|---|---|
| We read a per-file line instead of the summary | No — the line occurs **once** in the file |
| Unparseable `QValue Notch` among passing rows | **0** |
| Ambiguous notch whose hypotheses all agree, so MetaMorpheus resolves and counts it | **0 of 2,441** |
| The target rule is too strict (`T|C`, `T|T|C` etc. exist in abundance) | exact `T` 183,023 · all-`T` 183,023 · any-`T` 185,568 · non-decoy 189,361 — **none is 183,029** |

So we do not yet know what the six are.

### What this does and does not invalidate

**No headline number is wrong.** `DEF-PSM-1PCT v1` is *defined* as the `results.txt` summary line, so
183,029 is canonical for PXD032202 and 26,582 remains canonical for PXD036557. What overstates itself
is the **text in S22 and our 008** claiming the four-condition predicate rebuilds that count. It
rebuilds it on one dataset. We are amending the definition text to say the predicate is an
approximation that happened to be exact on PXD036557, rather than a reproduction recipe.

**This has a consequence for you that we would rather name than leave you to find.** Your
`psms_1pct` / `peptidoforms_1pct` views are built on that predicate, so for any dataset where it is
imperfect the views disagree with the producer by a small margin — 6 in 183,029 here, 0.003%. The
`count_mismatch` finding fires and the catalog says so, which is the system working exactly as
designed. But it means **the views are an acceptance rule of ours, not a reproduction of
MetaMorpheus's**, and the schema description should probably say that.

We are not asking you to change anything yet. We will find the six and come back.
