## 3 - DATAREPO-39: per-run enrichment

**PXD058611 is a mixed deposit.** From its own protocol: each sample was split, 300 µg processed directly
as a global proteome and 1,500 µg taken through the DCP-Bio1 persulfide probe and streptavidin capture.
There are 36 runs and roughly half are each. `Dataset.enrichment` is dataset-level, and your schema
describes it as "enrichment steps before MS", so `[none, chemical_probe]` would read as two steps in
sequence, which is wrong. We wrote `[chemical_probe]` plus a `mixed_enrichment` flag, and we will not
pool its intensities as whole proteome.

**Asked:** can enrichment be stated per run (or per assay), with the dataset value derived from it the
way your 004 derives `sdrf_status` from per-characteristic provenance? Where it comes from is the same
question as fractions: an SDRF column (`comment[enrichment process]` exists in the SDRF-Proteomics
spec) would carry it. So this may land together with the GeLC fraction work.
