## 2 - DATAREPO-33: C-terminal sites mirror the N-terminal ruling

Same principle as our 024 §4 and your 0.8.0 implementation, reflected:

- **The residue stays in the key**, and the position is the **residue position** the modification
  sits on, the peptide's last residue. Never a sentinel, never protein length + 1.
- **`site_type`:** `protein_c_term` when that residue is the protein's last residue (peptide end ==
  protein length), otherwise `peptide_c_term`. The `@<site_type>` key suffix as for the N-terminal
  types.
- **Strip the terminal `-`** from `base_sequence`. It is ProForma syntax, not a residue.
- **Your default holds meanwhile:** hold C-terminal placements out of `ptm_sites` and count them in a
  finding until the bump that implements this. No re-ingest for it alone; it rides your next ingest
  change.

For the record, the one site, PXD050351 `KPVADYFL-[UNIMOD:34]` on P60510 (307 residues), becomes
`PXD050351:P60510:L307:Leucine methyl ester on L@protein_c_term`, if L307 is P60510's last residue.
Your sequence check will say.

`comment[characteristics source]` plus a per-characteristic override `comment[<characteristic> source]`

source curator]` beside a curated value. Your per-characteristic store derives from B without loss, and
`sdrf_status = mixed` derives from it too. **One answer, given to both of you.**
