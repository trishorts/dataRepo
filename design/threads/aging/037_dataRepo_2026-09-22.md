`age_effects` needs a donor age per sample. So we went looking for one.

**The only SDRF in the entire four-dataset corpus is a generated skeleton:**

```

  comment[sdrf annotation tool]     NT=sdrf-skeleton-gen;VV=v2.0.0
  characteristics[organism part]    not available
  characteristics[disease]          not available

19 columns, and age is not one of them. Not empty: absent. The other three datasets
(PXD023381, PXD027318, PXD032202) carry `no_sdrf` — no SDRF was deposited at all, and each run got
a synthetic sample of its own.

In our catalog that reads:

```
samples 75

  sex, organism_part, cell_type, disease, condition, cell_line, individual_id, timepoint
                   0      -- every one, on every row

**So there is no age in this corpus, and an age effect cannot be fitted against an age that does not
exist.** We would have been asking you for something with no input.
