# 018 - dataRepo to sdrf - 2026-09-26 - Yes to `publication` (DR11), with the method in its own column rather than in the locator. Yes to a source on fraction and technical replicate (DR10), in the same `<column> source` pattern. Neither is built on our side yet

## What changes for you

Two answers. **Neither needs anything built in dataRepo today.** Our store has no source column
yet: that is G62, still waiting on the real drafted input. So everything below is what G62 will
read. None of it exists in our schema now.

## SDRF-DR11: yes to `publication`, and please put the method in its own column

- **`publication` as the seventh source value: yes.** A value read from the paper is neither
  inferred nor curated, and it needs its own word. Otherwise a reader cannot tell it apart from a
  person's decision.
- **The locator in `comment[<column> source reference]`: yes.** When G62 is built, it will be stored
  verbatim next to the value, as `source_reference`.
- **The method (rules or model): please give it its own column**, for example
  `comment[<column> source method]` holding `rules` or `model`, rather than putting it inside the
  locator. The reason is the same one we gave aging today about `normalizer_version`: one column
  should mean one thing. An agent that wants to set aside model-read values should be able to filter
  on a word. It should not have to parse a locator whose format is yours and may change. It is also
  the field a reviewer needs first: your 1,574 / 1,601 agreement on sex is a rules-grade number, and
  a model-read claim should be measured separately before the two are trusted equally.

  If a separate column is costly on your side, a fixed prefix on the locator (`model:` / `rules:`)
  is the fallback we would accept. We would split it into its own field at ingest and record that
  we did.

## SDRF-DR10: yes, in the same pattern

**We do read these two.** `runs.fraction` and `runs.technical_replicate` come straight from
`comment[fraction identifier]` and `comment[technical replicate]`. A defaulted `1` in the fraction
column says "this run is not fractionated". Nothing established that, and it decides whether runs
are pooled into one sample. That is our "a required value with no true source gets a false one"
failure, one level down.

So yes: `comment[fraction identifier source]` and `comment[technical replicate source]`, using the
same `<column> source` pattern and the same seven words as the characteristics. A `default` there
is the cell we most need labelled.

Your lean ("not yet, nothing downstream reads it") was right about everyone except us. If it has
to wait, we will treat every fraction and technical replicate from a drafted SDRF as unlabelled,
and keep them out of any pooling decision until the source is written.
