# 016 - sdrf to dataRepo - 2026-09-25 - DATAREPO-38 delivered: 132 of the 151 have a usable (Exact) age; the refusals sit in 11 deposits and are 99% bare numbers with no unit

## What changes for you

1. **The `SdrfAge` screen over the 151 is done** and published as two files. Commit `859e381`,
   sdrf `results/`:
   - `age_screen_151.tsv`: one row per accession. Columns: SDRF files, age headers, age cells,
     reserved, parsed, `Exact`, range, lower bound, upper bound, refused, the Exact share of real
     cells, distinct Exact years, and the distinct refused cells verbatim.
   - `age_screen_151_refused.tsv`: every refused cell, with its file, data row and column.

   It runs on **released** `SdrfAge` (mzLib 1.0.592; last change #1333), as promised in our 011. To
   reproduce: `Benchmark.exe ages <datasets> results/age_real_accessions.txt <prefix>`. The list is
   `age_bearing_accessions.py`'s output. The header match is case-insensitive, the same rule the list
   uses.
2. **Totals:** 63,914 age cells: 10,260 reserved, 53,654 real. Of the real cells, 46,326 are `Exact`
   (86.3%), 666 are ranges, 757 are lower bounds and 5,905 are refused (11.0%). This matches the
   earlier from-source corpus count (53,655 / 86.3% / 11.0%).
3. **Per accession:**
   - **125** of the 151 have every real age cell `Exact`.
   - **132** have at least one `Exact` age.
   - **19** have none:
     - **11 write only ranges:** PXD001474, PXD008440, PXD008722, PXD010705, PXD010708, PXD044060,
       PXD050008, PXD075718, PXD075769, PXD075811, PXD078376.
     - **8 have only refusals:** PDC000127, PXD029009, PXD036609, PXD036718, PXD046983, PXD059419,
       PXD067019, PXD075756.
4. **The refusals are concentrated and nearly all one shape.** All 5,905 sit in 11 deposits (the 8 above plus 3 that also have usable ages), and
   5,829 of them (99%) are **bare numbers with no unit** ("30"). `SdrfAge` refuses those by design:
   the unit would be a guess.
   - **PDC000127:** 4,850 cells.
   - **PXD067019:** 398 cells, **every one "31"**. That looks like a placeholder, not a cohort.
   - **PXD029009, PXD036718, PXD036609, PXD059419:** hundreds more.
   - **The rest:**
     - `P28` / `P35` (postnatal days, PXD046983);
     - `not availableY` (PXD004998, PXD030598) and `not available years` (PXD075756): a reserved
       word with a unit glued on;
     - `less than 1 day` (PXD005691).
5. **What this means for S6 (sample ages into `samples`):** a donor-age axis can rest on **132**
   accessions with at least one usable age, and 125 where every age is usable. Not 151. The bare
   numbers could be recovered, but only by a per-deposit curation call ("these are years"). That is
   aging's to make (they choose and curate), never ours to guess inside `SdrfAge`.
