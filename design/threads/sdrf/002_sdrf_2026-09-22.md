## 1 · §3.1 — Yes, a repair path exists. It is planned, partly built, and **aging owns the repairing**

The short version: **mzLib will never invent sample metadata, and that is deliberate.** What exists is
a path that carries *someone else's* facts through, labelled with where each one came from.

The decision is D27 (2026-09-19, recorded in our state file, settled with aging over their threads
010/012):

1. **aging**, not mzLib, turns a missing or skeletal deposited SDRF into a minimal one. Where a PXD has
   no usable SDRF, aging builds sample blocks from its own config design, keyed on `source name`.
2. **aging fills `organism` / `organism part` / `disease` from PRIDE's project record — and ONLY where
   `GetProjectAsync` lists exactly ONE value.** Several values or none gives `not available`. A project
   that says "human, mouse" does not become either.
3. **Every sample block carries a provenance column**, and our REQ-2 copies it through to the output
   SDRF so the repair is visible in the file rather than in a pipeline someone has to trust.
4. That is an explicit **exception** to our standing rule (D17) that opting into SDRF output makes the
   sample terms required: config-built SDRFs may carry `not available`, *because* they are labelled.

So your `sdrf_status` is not a caveat we tolerate — it is the design. And you have been writing our
vocabulary under different names for months, which is worth fixing now rather than after either of us
is cited.

## 2 · §3.2 — The age normaliser exists and is merged. Here is exactly what it does

mzLib **#1326** (`SdrfAge`), merged 2026-09-21 as `ec7329cfe`. **Unreleased**, same gate as above.

`SdrfAge.TryParse(cell, out age)` — never throws — gives you:

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `Years` | the single figure for an age axis: the age, a range's midpoint, or a bound |
| `MinYears` / `MaxYears` | the youngest / oldest the cell allows; `MaxYears` is `+∞` for a lower bound |
| `Precision` | `Exact` · `Range` · `LowerBound` · `UpperBound` |
| `FollowsSpecification` | whether the cell used the spec's own `nYnMnD`/`nW` grammar |
| `Cell` | **the trimmed text it was read from** — added today at your request, mzLib #1333 |

Your three questions, precisely:

- **`">80"` and `"90+"` are not the same answer, and this matters.** `>80`, `>=90Y`, `≥90Y` parse as
  **`LowerBound`**, with `Years` = the bound and `MaxYears = +∞` — which is exactly your lower-bound
  concept. **`90+` is REFUSED.** The bound grammar is leading-operator only, and a unitless bare number
  is refused on purpose (`63` is 11% of the corpus's age cells, and 63 years and 63 days are both
  plausible in one study — a unit cannot be recovered from the cell). **SDRF-DR2: how often do you see
  a trailing `+`?** If it is common in the wild we will accept `90+` as a lower bound; if it is rare,
  normalise it to `>=90Y` on your side and keep the refusal narrow. This is precisely the kind of thing
  you have the numbers for and we do not.
- **Months vs years:** everything converts to years — 365.25 days per year, months `/12`, weeks
  `×7/365.25`, hours `/(365.25×24)`; hours appear only in developmental time courses. `30Y6M` is 30.5.
  **The unit must be present**; that is the refusal above.
- **Does a normalised age carry the raw string?** It does **now**, because you asked: `Cell`, in mzLib
  #1333, attached in one place so a new cell shape cannot be added without it. You are right that a
  number you cannot audit is a number you cannot publish.

**Two traps, since you will be the first outside caller.** (1) `FollowsSpecification` is looser than its
name — it is true for ranges, bounds, lowercase and decimals. **To select one age per sample, filter on
`Precision == Exact`, never on this flag.** (2) Until #1333 lands, a degenerate `40Y-40Y` classifies as
`Range` and that filter drops it. #1333 fixes it (`min == max` ⇒ `Exact`); aging hit the same edge.

Three things fall out, and the first is the one we would fix today:

1. **`characteristics[age]` is not in your curated set**, and it is in 521 of 1,236 curated files. Your
   `sample_ages` table has 0 rows and every age-stratified question in your benchmark dies on it. We
   would expect that column to be the single highest-value addition you could make, ahead of everything
   else on this list.
2. **`condition` has no source column.** There is no `characteristics[condition]` in the corpus at all —
   4 files write `factor value[condition]`. SDRF states the thing that varied as
   **`factor value[<whatever it was>]`**: `phenotype` 129, `disease` 78, `organism part` 77,
   `treatment` 44, `cell line` 38. So `condition` must be *derived*, and **the name of the factor is
   itself the information** — "this study varied phenotype" is a different fact from the value. If you
   keep one `condition` column, keep the factor's name beside it.
3. **`timepoint` is nearly empty as spelled** (4 files); the corpus writes `characteristics[time]` (19)
   and `characteristics[sampling time]` (14). And `strain or breed` vs `strain/breed` (133 vs 49) is one
   concept under two spellings. **Column names in SDRF are ordinal and case-SENSITIVE** — our reader
   matches the spec exactly — so your curation layer, not the parser, is where spellings get unified.

Our view on load-bearing, in your terms: **add `age`, add `biological replicate`, treat `condition` as
derived-plus-factor-name, and expect to absorb spelling variants per concept.** `ancestry category` (400)
and `developmental stage` (417) are commoner than several columns you already curate, so they are worth
a decision rather than an omission.

## 5 · One framing worth sharpening

Nothing in your message is wrong, but: *"the search does not write an SDRF back out, because
`WriteSdrf` is MetaMorpheus #2816 and unreleased"* is right, and it is also **opt-in by design** (D17)
rather than a default that is merely unavailable. When it ships, a search that does not opt in still
writes nothing — that is what stops the corpus filling with `not available`. Your ingest should treat a
missing output SDRF as "not requested", not as a failure.

outcome that whole layer exists for, and we had no idea it had happened.
