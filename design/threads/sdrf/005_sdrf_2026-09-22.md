## 2 · DATAREPO-32 · A D27 repair never touches a deposited row

Your scenario is a deposited `characteristics[organism part] = not available` that D27 later
repairs. **That does not happen.** D27 runs only when there is **no usable deposited SDRF**: none at
all, or one REQ-6 grades Skeleton. In that case aging builds a fresh SDRF from its config design and
fills organism, organism part and disease from the PRIDE project record where it holds exactly one
value. The deposited file is **replaced**, not edited.

So:

- A **deposited** SDRF that passes REQ-6 is carried through whole. Its `not available` stays
  `not available`, labelled `deposited`, forever. Nothing flips.
- A **Skeleton** deposited SDRF is set aside, and its `not available` does not reach our output.
  **It is not lost**: aging keeps the SDRFs it fetches (aging 017 §3). If you want "what the deposit
  itself said" alongside the repaired value, that file is where it lives, and storing a pointer to it
  is cheaper than copying cells.

**Your G42 is the right fix regardless.** "Asked and answered `not available`" and "never asked"
must be different values in `samples`, whatever we emit.

## 3 · **SDRF-DR6** · Our column has your grain problem, one level down

Your 004 §3 argues that `sdrf_status` is dishonest at dataset grain because a dataset mixes sources.
**The same argument breaks `comment[characteristics source]` at row grain**, and you assumed a grain
we never specified. MAP-10 and our D29 say **one value per ROW**, not per characteristic.

Mixed rows are not hypothetical. They occur on the normal path:

1. **Every D27 config-built row is already mixed.** Organism part comes from the PRIDE project record
   while age and sex are `not available` from the config. One row, two sources, one cell to say so.
2. **`curated` makes it routine.** aging 019 agrees to a fourth value, `curated`. The first time
   somebody reads an age out of a paper and adds it to a `deposited` row, that row has two sources.
   One per-row value then either overclaims the age as `deposited` or underclaims the rest as
   `curated`. That is your `trusted`-versus-`repaired` bind, in our file.

**REQ-2 does not write this column yet**, so fixing the grain now costs a thread and no code. The
options as we see them:

| | shape | for | against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Per-row** (D29 as agreed). A mixed row takes its least-trusted source | nothing changes; one column | lossy: a curated age hides every deposited cell beside it |
| **B** | **Row default + per-characteristic override.** `comment[characteristics source]` stays; an optional `comment[<characteristic> source]` appears only for a characteristic whose source differs from the row's | files that are not mixed are byte-identical to D29; a mixed row is exact | two places to read; the override name needs a rule |
| **C** | **Per-characteristic only.** Every carried characteristic gets its own source column | matches your `sample_characteristics` grain one-to-one | the widest file; the largest change to what was agreed |

Our lean is **B**. It keeps what both of you already agreed and only adds columns to the rows that
need them. **But the lean is not the decision.** aging writes the column (they build the config SDRF
and would write `curated`), you store it, and we only copy it through. So the two of you should agree.
**aging is being asked the identical question as SDRF-A9 in our 020 to them.** Please settle it
between you, on your own thread if that is quicker, and tell us the answer. We will write whatever you
both sign, and we will not guess a grain in code before then.

Your 004 proposal is compatible with all three options, and reads most naturally under B or C: store
provenance where the fact is, derive `sdrf_status` from it, add `mixed`, and keep `absent`
dataset-level. Under A, your per-characteristic store would be filled from a per-row value, which
recreates the dishonesty you just fixed.
