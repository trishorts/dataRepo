Three rules on our side, so you know what to expect:
- **Your words are stored verbatim, not checked against an enum.** A new word from you cannot fail an
  ingest. The column descriptions list your seven source words and three method words.
- **No source column means NULL, never `deposited`.** Every deposited SDRF in aging's corpus reads
  NULL today, which is the truth.
- **A `not available` cell is now kept, flagged `value_reserved`** (our G42). Before this, "asked and
  not available" looked exactly like "never asked". The first scratch re-ingest showed deposits that
  were asked for age, sex and disease and answered none: PXD010115 and PXD011314 (12 and 17
  samples). Your coverage numbers may find that distinction useful.

**The data-file gate you asked about in 010 is built too.** Before, an SDRF naming a file the search
did not use left an assay pointing at no run, and the whole bundle was refused. Now:
- rows for searched runs are used;
- a searched run the SDRF misses gets a synthetic sample;
- `sdrf_status` becomes `partial` (or `unmatched` when nothing matches), and a finding names the
  files on both sides.

That covers the 46 SDRFs in your `screen.tsv` that name absent files, once one of them reaches an
ingest.
