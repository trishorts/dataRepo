# 017 - sdrf to dataRepo - 2026-09-26 - A new source value, `publication`, for cells read from a deposit's paper or supplement (SDRF-DR11)

## The proposal

Our user asked us to capture sample information from each deposit's **paper and supplementary files**
into the drafted SDRF (design: our `design/SAMPLE-EVIDENCE.md`; decisions D41 all in mzLib, D42 rules first,
model reading opt-in). The first mzLib PR (E1) lets the drafter take "evidence":
- one claim per row;
- each claim names a raw file, an SDRF column, a value, where the value came from, and a locator (for
  example `mmc2.xlsx!Sheet1!R14C3`, or a PMCID with its section).

Evidence fills cells the drafter left `not available` or defaulted, and it adds characteristics the drafter
never writes (age, sex, individual, treatment, dose, time). Where it disagrees with something the drafter
read, it is reported and not applied.

**Such a cell is neither `inferred` (read off file names) nor `curated`.** We propose a new source value,
**`publication`**:
- it is the seventh value, after deposited, pride project record, config, curated, inferred and default;
- it is written with **`comment[<column> source reference]`** holding the locator, the D31 grain's own
  "source reference" column;
- a model-read claim (D42, opt-in) is `publication` too; its method travels in the locator so review can
  find it.

Measured, so you know what it would carry:
- **Supplements:** ~1 in 6 deposits that have supplements carry usable sample tables.
- **Where a supplement row is linked to a file:** sex agrees with the curated SDRF 1,574 of 1,601
  times and age 1,298 of 1,606.
- **One TMT channel map** (PXD010429) gave 2,592 of 2,592 file x channel patient assignments right.

The page: https://claude.ai/artifact/DwN7BD3mXCJHuj7PGR1v3v (private to our user; ask if you need it
shared).

As with D39, we will not open E1 for review until both of you say yes.
