## 1 - REQ-DATAREPO-8: (b), and why it is not (a)

Your 011 read our 009 §6 correctly: *we ingest what the producer writes, and we never resolve it
ourselves*. Option (a) would keep the logic in mzLib. But we would be the ones running it, choosing
the gene-set release and deciding when to re-run. That makes us the producer of a scientific
result, which our D1 rules out. We store and serve.

Under (b) nothing is bent:

- **You own the logic, the run and the release choice.** A new Ensembl release is your re-run and
  a new delivery, not our code change.
- **Provenance comes out right by construction.** Every row already carries
  `search_database_sha256`, `gene_set_release` and `gene_set_sha256`, so a row traces to your
  delivery, and your delivery traces to Ensembl.
- **One delivery per database, not per dataset.** Your §1 argument holds on our side too. The
  nine datasets sharing `760984e8...` join to one table.

When the pyMzLib verb exists, nothing changes. It becomes how *you* run it.

## 2 - REQ-DATAREPO-9: the shape, accepted

- **Long format, one row per (accession, gene), never a pick.** Yes. It is the same rule as our
  `organelle_term_categories` for go and `trait_effects` for ptmQtl: nothing is ever a list paired
  by position.
- **Key `(search_database_sha256, gene_set_release, accession, gene_stable_id)`.** Accepted. The
  four outcomes with no gene (`not_in_source`, `unrecognized_accession`,
  `contaminant_not_mapped`, and `off_primary_only` if you write no gene row for it) are one row
  with a NULL `gene_stable_id`. A NULL in a key is fine for us as long as there is exactly one
  such row per accession. Please confirm that holds.
- **`uniprot_gene_name` as a label only.** Yes. And to repeat our 009 §3 so it is on this thread
  too: **nothing from this table is ever back-filled into `Protein.gene`**, which stays verbatim
  and unvalidated (D9). A disagreement between the two is a QC signal (G48), and your table is
  what makes it countable.
- **`contaminant_not_mapped` as its own outcome** is exactly your 002 §0. A contaminant is never
  mapped through orthology. We are glad it is a first-class value and not a missing row.

**DATAREPO-L1: what is the outcome column called, and what does a decoy get?** Your §3 lists six

**DATAREPO-L2: the contaminant database.** aging's searches read two databases: the human proteome
