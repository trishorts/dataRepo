# 021 - dataRepo to logs - 2026-09-25 - Your rows now have a home: `gene_resolutions` in datarepo 0.20.0, filled by the operator through `datarepo run logs.resolve_genes`

## What changes for you

1. **datarepo 0.20.0 (`bb2b365`) stores your resolution**, as table `gene_resolutions`. It has your
   verb's columns under mzLib's names, plus `definition_id = logs:DEF-GENE-RESOLUTION v1` on every row.
   pyMzLib's `source_index` / `source_path` are not stored; `search_database_sha256` says the same
   thing portably.
2. **Who runs it:** the instance operator (aging), with `datarepo run logs.resolve_genes`. We do not
   run it to serve. Our runs are scratch tests.
3. **What the runner checks against your manifest**, refusing on any failure. This is the check you
   asked for in 016 §1:
   - the gene set file's sha256 must be a species' `inputs.gene_set.sha256`, and the xref's must be
     the one paired with it;
   - every row's `gene_set_sha256`, `ensembl_xref_sha256` and `gene_set_release` must equal that
     species' `row_values`;
   - the xref is required, so every stored row is v1 (017 §3).
4. **One resolution per searched target database**, shared by every dataset that searched it. The
   contaminant database is never passed in, as agreed in our 018.

## 1 - What we saw on the released path

A scratch run through the real CLI, from a clean clone at `bb2b365` with `mzlib==0.2.0` from PyPI:
- the human proteome gave **20,899 rows**, the same count as your reference;
- the rat proteome gave **9,048 rows**, with the outcome counts of our 020;
- rat's extra isoform database got its own 17-row artefact.

The stored key `(search_database_sha256, gene_set_sha256, gene_set_release, accession, gene_id)` is
unique on both. It is your key with the GTF's sha256 beside the release, because two gene sets can
share a release number.

**The join (LOGS-D2, entry level):** a catalog table `protein_genes` joins each dataset's target
proteins to the rows for a database that dataset actually searched. It covered 3,774 of 3,774 human
and 5,025 of 5,025 rat target proteins. Contaminant proteins are excluded, and so is any protein whose
contaminant flag is unknown.

## 2 - What would change it

- **A new pyMzLib column**: the runner refuses it until our schema takes it. Please tell us before a
  release adds one.
- **Your proteoform-to-entry rule (LOGS-D2)**: not needed on today's corpus (019 §2). When a search
  reports variant accessions, we will ask you for it before joining them.
