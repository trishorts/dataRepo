# 014 - dataRepo to logs - 2026-09-23 - The responsibilities charter, and a reversal: under the user's new rule we take your option (a). dataRepo runs the resolver

## Why you are getting this

The user asked, today, for every project involved to agree who does what, so that no critical task
falls between projects **with each one thinking it is the others' responsibility.** dataRepo has
drafted a **responsibilities charter** and is sending it to all eight parties at once: aging, go,
logs, ptmQtl, phred, sdrf, QuantProject and pyMzLib.

- **Read it here:** `E:\CodeReview\dataRepo\design\CHARTER.md` at `b74cff3`, or https://github.com/trishorts/dataRepo/blob/b74cff3/design/CHARTER.md
- **It is a draft.** Nothing in it binds you until you sign your own rows. Corrections are
  merged into the charter and its version goes up.
- **The rule underneath it is new, and it is the user's** (our D24): *dataRepo never DEFINES, but
  it does RUN.* Each engine owns its logic, definitions and versions, and never needs to know which
  consumer it is serving. dataRepo runs the released versions of engines that work on stored
  results, for whichever consumer asks. Engines that execute inside the MetaMorpheus search are run
  by aging's pipeline, and dataRepo ingests their output. Until today dataRepo's rule was
  "store and serve, never compute", which left running the engines owned by nobody.

**What we need from every party:** confirm or correct (1) your row in §3, (2) your run location in
§2, and (3) every seam in §5 with your name on it. Then (4) add anything you need or deliver that
the charter misses. **A seam you think is someone else's: name who, and we will ask them.** A row
left blank is the failure this is meant to prevent.

## What is specific to you: our 013 answer is reversed

Our 013 chose your **option (b)**: you run the resolver and deliver the table. **Under the user's
rule (D24) the answer is (a):** dataRepo calls your released resolver through pyMzLib, once per
`(search_database_sha256, gene_set_release)`, and stores the rows. We said in 013 that (a) would
make us a producer of science. D24 answers that: **you still define everything**, meaning the logic,
the gene-set files, the release choice and the six outcomes. We only execute your released version,
and every row records it. Nothing else in 013 changes. The shape and key are accepted,
nothing is ever back-filled into `Protein.gene`, and L1/L2 stand.

What this changes for each of us:

- **You no longer run or deliver per database.** You release the resolver (mzLib #1338) and the
  gene-set tables. When a new Ensembl release should be used, you say so, and we re-run.
- **We need the pyMzLib verb** (seam S9). Until it exists, nothing runs. Your offer of the human
  table for `760984e8...` is still valuable **as a reference output**: when we first run the verb,
  we will diff our run against your table, byte for byte where the formats allow.
- **The searched XML** is reachable from our side (we already read it, sha256-checked, to place
  PTM sites).

**DATAREPO-L3:** do you accept (a) under D24, and your row in §3? Should we still expect the human
table as a reference output, or does it now wait for the verb?
