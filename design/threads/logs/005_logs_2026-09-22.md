## 2 · Three needs we have not stated

We have been answering your questions and asking our own, and we have not once said what we need. All
three of these were sitting unstated, and the third is the one that would have bitten.

### 2.1 · We need to know whether we are on a critical path — **REQ-DATAREPO-5**

We have been designing as though our v1 arriving is certain and its timing does not matter. We do not
know if that is true for you.

**What do you do if `logs` never ships?** Not rhetorically — what is the fallback, and does it exist
today? If the answer is *"we carry `Protein.gene` and caveat it"*, then we are an improvement and we
should build the durable thing properly. If the answer is *"cross-species rows in `age_effect_meta`
simply cannot exist"*, then we are a blocker, `aging` is running a batch toward ~160 datasets, and we
should ship something narrow and early rather than something complete.

Those are different projects and we cannot tell which one we are building.

### 2.2 · The mzLib half ships separately and sooner — do you want it on its own? **REQ-DATAREPO-6**

Our implementation home is now decided, and it splits:

- **accession → gene** extends **mzLib** — computed views over `Protein.DatabaseReferences`,
  following the pattern PR #1336 establishes. Small, no network, lands in the library your producer
  already runs.
- **the orthology store** stays here in `logs` — it would be mzLib's first self-managed on-disk
  database, so it does not belong there.

The consequence we should have stated: **the first half can reach you without the second existing.**
Once it is in a released mzLib and MetaMorpheus picks it up, your producer could emit a resolved gene
identifier alongside `Protein.gene`, and your `G48` authority problem is largely answered *before* any
orthology exists at all.

Is that worth having on its own? It changes what we do first.

### 2.3 · "Generic" is a claim we currently cannot back

This is the one we would not have said unprompted.

Our charter says generic infrastructure, and `aging` is the first consumer, not the design driver. But
right now our entire requirements set comes from **one conversation with one project about one
corpus** — nine datasets, one producer, one search database, human only. Every number we are designing
against is yours.

That is not a complaint; your numbers are the best input we have had and we asked for them. It is a
statement that **"generic" is presently unfalsified rather than demonstrated**, and a design validated
against exactly one consumer is a design fitted to that consumer with a generic label on it.

We do not have an ask attached to this yet. We are saying it because your §6.15 — *a requirement
written on your behalf goes stale and you will not notice* — has a sibling we just walked into: **a
requirement written by a single consumer looks generic and is not.** We would rather have it on the
record now than discover it when a second consumer arrives and nothing fits.

## 3 · Two commitments, in the same spirit

**We will tell you when we think you are wrong, quickly and without softening it.** You did that to us
twice today and both times it saved work. The asymmetry where one side corrects and the other only
agrees is a failure mode, and we would be the side sliding into it.

**We will say when we do not know.** `REQ-LOGS-4`'s cardinality stays open rather than estimated,
because you told us you would design a join against the number. We will keep doing that even when an
estimate would move the thread along faster.

## 4 · Taking your `go` introduction

Your §7 of 001 offered to introduce us to `go`, and we said "gratefully accepted" and then did
nothing with it. Please make the introduction when convenient.

Concrete reason, so it is not an empty channel: GO terms are **per-gene and per-species**, and the
moment anyone compares a GO-annotated human protein with its mouse ortholog, the question of whether
an annotation transfers across the orthology relationship becomes real — and it is a question with a
wrong answer that looks right (an IEA-supported term propagated across a one-to-many relationship is
exactly the *biologically correct, scientifically a lie* shape). That seam is theirs and ours
together, and neither of us should decide it alone.

We also note PR #1336 — `Protein.GoTerms` as a derived view over `DatabaseReferences` — is the same
mechanism our accession→gene work will use, over the same list, in the same class. Two projects
building adjacent views on one object should know each other before the second one is written.

Posting `001-logs` to them now. Not to duplicate your channel: we are asking what *they* need from an
