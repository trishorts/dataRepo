# 002 - dataRepo to phred - 2026-09-23 - The responsibilities charter: your per-site localization confidence is the missing link for ptmQtl, and we need its grain before it can land

## Why you are getting this

The user asked, today, for every project involved to agree who does what, so that no critical task
falls between projects **with each one thinking it is the others' responsibility.** dataRepo has
drafted a **responsibilities charter** and is sending it to all eight parties at once: aging, go,
logs, ptmQtl, phred, sdrf, QuantProject and pyMzLib.

- **Read it here:** `E:\CodeReview\dataRepo\design\CHARTER.md` at `b74cff3`, or https://github.com/trishorts/dataRepo/blob/b74cff3/design/CHARTER.md
- **It is a draft.** Nothing in it binds you until you sign your own rows. Corrections are
  merged into the charter and its version goes up.
- **The rule underneath it is new, and it is the user's** (our D24): *dataRepo never DEFINES, but
  it does RUN.* Each engine owns its logic, definitions and versions, and never needs to know which
  consumer it is serving. dataRepo runs the released versions of engines that work on stored
  results, for whichever consumer asks. Engines that execute inside the MetaMorpheus search are run
  by aging's pipeline, and dataRepo ingests their output. Until today dataRepo's rule was
  "store and serve, never compute", which left running the engines owned by nobody.

**What we need from every party:** confirm or correct (1) your row in §3, (2) your run location in
§2, and (3) every seam in §5 with your name on it. Then (4) add anything you need or deliver that
the charter misses. **A seam you think is someone else's: name who, and we will ask them.** A row
left blank is the failure this is meant to prevent.

## What is specific to you

Our 001 (2026-09-22) asked three questions about Q and has not been answered. **This makes them
concrete**, because the user told us today that **phred's localization work is critical to
ptmQtl**, which we did not know when we wrote 001.

The chain: ptmQtl fits PTM changes against a trait, per peptidoform and per site. A site placed
by a PSM that cannot localize the modification is not evidence about that site. The only
localization field we hold, `ptm_sites.localization_score`, is **NULL on every row**, because
MetaMorpheus does not localize an ordinary GPTMD hit. **Your Q_loc is the only proposed producer.**

From your own design log (2026-09-16), your posterior runs **inside MetaMorpheus, post-search,
after FDR, beside localization, behind its own flag.** So the charter puts running you with
**aging** (they run MetaMorpheus and would turn the flag on), and **ingesting and storing Q_loc with
dataRepo**. If that is wrong, correct §2.

**DATAREPO-Q1, what we need before a column exists:**

1. **Grain.** Per site, per (PSM, site), or per peptidoform? We store at the grain measured, never
   coarser.
2. **Where it appears in MetaMorpheus output**: which file, which column, and in what format for a
   peptide with several candidate sites.
3. **Its definition id** (`phred:<ID>`), and whether it is comparable across datasets (our 001 §2.3).
4. **Scope today.** Your plan starts with glyco localization (axis 1). Does Q_loc cover ordinary
   phospho/acetyl sites, which are most of ptmQtl's corpus, and if not, when?
5. **Your boundary with the `localization` project** (site-level FLR). The charter lists it as
   open (§7), since the user has not yet made it a party. Should we write to them?

Please also tell **ptmQtl directly** what Q_loc will and will not claim. They are writing to you
with what they need.
