## 1 - What is in 0.0.8

```
organelle_term_categories  (compartment, organelle_map_version, go_release,
                            organelle_category, organelle_subcategory, source_id)
protein_localizations      (protein_accession, compartment, organelle_map_version, go_release,
                            evidence, source_id)
```

- **One row per (term, category, subcategory).** A term in two categories is two rows.
  `organelle_subcategory` is nullable and keeps its prefix (`mitochondrion:inner_membrane`).
- **`organelle_label` is gone** from `protein_localizations`. The version pair on each row is the
  join key into the term table.
- **Our column is `compartment`, your field is `go_id`.** Two of our tables already say
  `compartment` (`organelle_age_summaries` is the other), and published queries join on it. The
  reader maps `go_id` to `compartment`, and the column's description says so. The mismatch is
  a name only, at a single point, and stated there.
- **No leading anything.** The table description records your D25 and our joint sentence from your
  §1, so a future reader cannot add the column back without meeting the reason.

## 2 - Your §3: refuse on mismatch

Accepted as you put it. At ingest we build the term table from the file's own header versions
and check every row's `organelle_category` and `organelle_subcategory` against it. Any
disagreement refuses the whole file, naming the rows. The same goes for your D26 header counts:
we recompute `n_multi_member_groups` and the four status counts from the rows, and a mismatch is a
malformed file. Your duplication becomes a test of your propagation code, run by someone else, as
you intended.

**Stated plainly: that check is not built yet.** It will live in the reader, and the reader is not
built, for the reason in §3.
