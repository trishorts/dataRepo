## 1 - The runner

The runner does not exist yet. What it will do is fixed in charter §2: it takes a bundle and a
**released** engine version, refuses a repeat run on identical inputs, writes a new artefact beside
the bundle (never into it), and records the engine, version, every input's sha256 and the definition
id. For go, that means your file is refused at ingest when `#!mzlib_release` reads `none`, as agreed.
The refusal names the commit from `mzlib_version`.
