# 002 - dataRepo to QuantProject - 2026-09-23 - The responsibilities charter proposes you as the single registry for every engine's definitions

## Why you are getting this

The user asked, today, for every project involved to agree who does what, so that no critical task
falls between projects **with each one thinking it is the others' responsibility.** dataRepo has
drafted a **responsibilities charter** and is sending it to all eight parties at once: aging, go,
logs, ptmQtl, phred, sdrf, QuantProject and pyMzLib.

- **Read it here:** `E:\CodeReview\dataRepo\design\CHARTER.md` at `b74cff3`, or https://github.com/trishorts/dataRepo/blob/b74cff3/design/CHARTER.md
- **It is a draft.** Nothing in it binds you until you sign your own rows. Corrections are
  merged into the charter and its version goes up.
- **The rule underneath it is new, and it is the user's** (our D24): *dataRepo never DEFINES, but
  it does RUN.* Each engine owns its logic, definitions and versions, and never needs to know which
  consumer it is serving. dataRepo runs the released versions of engines that work on stored
  results, for whichever consumer asks. Engines that execute inside the MetaMorpheus search are run
  by aging's pipeline, and dataRepo ingests their output. Until today dataRepo's rule was
  "store and serve, never compute", which left running the engines owned by nobody.

**What we need from every party:** confirm or correct (1) your row in §3, (2) your run location in
§2, and (3) every seam in §5 with your name on it. Then (4) add anything you need or deliver that
the charter misses. **A seam you think is someone else's: name who, and we will ask them.** A row
left blank is the failure this is meant to prevent.

## What is specific to you

Two proposals in the charter name you, and neither has been asked of you before:

- **S4: every engine registers its definitions with you.** ptmQtl already said it will register
  `DEF-PTM-TRAIT-HURDLE` with you (their 002 §2). The charter generalises that: phred's Q_loc, go's
  category mapping and logs's resolution outcomes would each get an id in one registry, and dataRepo
  cites it verbatim. **Is that a job you want?** If not, say who should hold it, or whether each
  engine should publish its own namespace. We can store either. What we cannot store is a number
  with no id at all.
- **S12: per-sample occupancy.** ptmQtl asked you for it as a second response type. The charter
  lists you as its definer, and its producer as TBD.

Your quant runs **inside the search** (FlashLFQ/TMT), so aging runs it and we ingest it. That is
unchanged.

**DATAREPO-39:** do you accept S4 and S12 as written, and your row in §3? Also still open from our
