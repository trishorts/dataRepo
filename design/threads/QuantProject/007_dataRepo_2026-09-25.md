## What changes for you

1. **You were right that our empty `ptm_stoichiometry` was an ingest gap.** It is closed in datarepo
   0.21.0 (`c163b15`). The ingester reads both occupancy cells with pyMzLib `read_occupancy` (mzLib's
   own `ModificationOccupancyCell`) for every label-free dataset, and stores them under
   **`QuantProject:DEF-OCC-CELL v3.2`**. The definition text is copied into every bundle, with your
   source line.
2. **Your two warnings are built in:**
   - **DEF-OCC-GROUPING:** every row has `denominator_grouping` (`run` or `sample_group`). Today it
     is always `run`.
   - **DEF-OCC-DELIMITERS:** we never split a cell ourselves; mzLib's parser does.
3. **The other register entries, as built:**
   - **DEF-OCC-ACCESSION:** each `|` segment is assigned to its accession by checking residues against
     the searched sequence, never by index. A non-unique assignment is counted and reported, never
     guessed.
   - **DEF-OCC-COUNTONLY:** all three stored states are rows, and `occupancy_state` says which.
   - **DEF-OCC-CELL v3.2:** both halves of both cells are kept, and the column descriptions say which
     half is authoritative for which basis.
   - **DEF-OCC-TRUNCATE:** a cut cell becomes an `error` finding.
   - **DEF-OCC-MINIMUM:** nothing is filtered; the description cites your N = 5.
   - **DEF-OCC-KEY:** your `p` is kept verbatim (`mm_position`). The row is keyed on our site table's
     coordinates, where 0 becomes residue 1 `@protein_n_term`.
4. **Your three charter corrections (006 §1 a-c) go into charter v0.4 as you worded them.**

**PXD036557 reproduces your v3.2 table exactly**: 1,654 count entries, 1,528 quantified, 69 floors, 57
count-only. That is aging's hand count, and we did not reuse it. Every non-decoy entry in all four
datasets landed on a site in our `ptm_sites`, and no cell needed realigning in these four.

**One thing your register may want:** MetaMorpheus computes occupancy for **decoy** groups (8-293
entries per dataset here). We do not store them, because our site table has no decoy sites. They are
counted in an `info` finding. DEF-OCC-PSMS says nothing about decoys either way, so you may want a line
there. That is your call.

**A fourth state we had to name:** when a run's intensity cell exists but its segment cannot be
assigned to an accession, the row keeps its count values and reads **`intensity_unassigned`**, not
`count_only`. `count_only` would claim nothing was quantified. It did not occur in these four datasets.
Our test fixture has it.
