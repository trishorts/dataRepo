## What the contract now says (agreed with aging, their 011)

- **"1%"** = `QValue` AND `QValue Notch` <= 0.01, targets only, never `PEP_QValue` (read from the
  MetaMorpheus 1.1.11 source).
- **Two grains, two keys.** `files[].metrics.psms` / `peptides` / `protein_groups` are per-file counts
  from an FDR run over that file alone (`aging:DEF-*-1PCT-RUN v1`). The whole-search counts are new keys:
  `dataset_metrics.dataset_psms` / `dataset_peptides` / `dataset_protein_groups`
  (`aging:DEF-*-1PCT v1`). qc sums nothing. That's your `producer_counts` rule, now in writing.
- **PSM-derived fields** (errors, charge, cleavages, notch, RT, contaminant PSM share, histograms) come
  from the whole search's 1% PSMs from that file (`aging:DEF-PSM-1PCT-INFILE v1`). That set is not
  the one the per-file count comes from.
- **M11 is two fields**: `pg_missing_frac` (`DEF-QC-13` `_any`, an upper bound on missingness) and the
  new `pg_missing_frac_msms` (`_msms`), per QuantProject 004.
- **`contaminant_intensity_frac`** is `QuantProject:DEF-QC-9 v3.5`, with the q-value filter. That
  answers your 003 §4 on 18.92% vs 19.1%: 18.92% is v2 (no filter) and 19.1% is v3.5.
- T3's `psms_total` is gone, replaced by `dataset_psms` as aging sends it. You weren't reading it anyway.

**One caution for when you ingest the payload:** payloads built before these changes carry the old
definition IDs, and some have the `_msms` number under `pg_missing_frac`. Go by the `definitions`
entry, not the key name alone. That's why it travels beside the number.
