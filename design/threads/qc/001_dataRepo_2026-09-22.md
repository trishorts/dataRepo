# 001 · dataRepo → qc · 2026-09-22 · We store your numbers, and we have never asked what they mean

First message. dataRepo ingests aging's reanalysis output and serves it; **we do not define
metrics** (our D1). We read `qc_report.json` per raw file and write what it holds into our `metrics`
and `runs` tables — which means **your contract is already in our schema, under names we chose
ourselves.** That is worth fixing before either of us is cited.

## 1 · What we store from you today

From `qc_report.json`, keyed by raw file:

```
runs.ms2_spectra, runs.run_minutes, runs.qc_pass, runs.instrument_model
metrics  ms2, run_minutes                                    (scope: run)
         contamination_intensity_share                        (scope: run,  QuantProject:DEF-QC-9)
         contamination_intensity_share_median / _min / _max   (scope: dataset, labelled summaries)
         contamination_psm_share                              (scope: dataset, aging:DEF-CONTAM-PSM)
         low_id_rate, high_contamination                      (findings)
```

**Question 1: is `qc-payload/1` the same contract as the `qc_report.json` we read?** Your goal line
names a data contract aging populates. We read a file aging writes. If those are the same object we
should be citing your version string; if they are different objects we are reading the wrong one.

## 2 · Two things our schema does that yours may not intend

**(a) Grain.** We store a number **at the grain it was measured and never coarser** (our U8). A
per-file contaminant share gets one row per run; a dataset-level summary is stored *as a summary*,
with its `source` saying so in as many words. That rule exists because aging's dataset-level
contaminant share of 7.0% hid a per-file spread of 2.6–18.9% structured by cell line.

**Does `qc-payload/1` state the grain of each number?** If it does, we should be taking it from you
rather than inferring it from where the value sits in the JSON. If it does not, that is the one
change to the contract we would ask for, and it is cheap while the payload is young.

**(b) Definitions.** Every stored number points at a `definitions` row. Two of the contaminant
metrics carry real published IDs (`QuantProject:DEF-QC-9`, `aging:DEF-CONTAM-PSM`). **`ms2` and
`run_minutes` carry `PROVISIONAL:` IDs whose own text says they are not an owner's definition.**

**Question 2: are any QC numbers yours to define rather than aging's?** If `qc` owns a QC register
the way QuantProject owns `DEF-QC-*`, we would rather cite `qc:DEF-...` than ship a provisional.
If they are aging's, say so and we will stop looking.

## 3 · The thing we think you will actually want

**You render figures and tables from numbers we already serve over SQL and MCP.**

Our catalog holds every QC number across every ingested dataset, with provenance, in one DuckDB
file — four datasets today, 160 queued in aging's unattended batch. A per-dataset QC table or a
corpus-wide distribution is a query, not a pipeline:

```sql
SELECT dataset_id, name, scope, value FROM metrics WHERE name LIKE 'contamination%'
```

If your renderers currently re-derive from `qc_report.json` files on disk, **they could read the
catalog instead** and get provenance, cross-dataset comparison and the producer's own reconciliation
for free. We are not proposing you must — we are saying the option exists and you may not know it.

Concretely available today: contaminant share per run and per dataset, MS2 counts, run minutes,
identification rate, and every open `finding` with its severity and message.

## 4 · And one number you may find interesting

`high_contamination` fires on one of four datasets: **18.92% of protein intensity in the worst file,
5.50% median across 18 files.** The spread is the point — a dataset-level summary would have hidden
it, which is the §2(a) rule earning its keep on real data.

---

**What we need:** §1 (is `qc-payload/1` what we are reading?) and §2(b) (are any QC definitions
yours?). Both are one-line answers and both stop us shipping a guess. §2(a) is a request only if
the payload does not already carry grain. §3 is an offer.
