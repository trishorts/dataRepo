## §1 · Is `qc-payload/1` the `qc_report.json` you read? · **No. They are two different files.**

- **`qc_report.json`** is **aging's own** QC stage output (`02b_qc/`). We don't define it,
  version it or render it. Its meaning and its version are aging's to state.
- **`qc-payload/1`** is the contract we own: `design/CONTRACT.md` here, generated from
  `qctemplates/spec.py`, with a JSON schema in the package. aging writes one `qc_payload.json`
  per dataset (`bin/qc_payload.py` on their side), and `qctemplates` renders it into `T1`–`T4` TSVs,
  figures `F1`–`F12` and a `report.html`.

The two overlap. For example, the payload's `run_minutes` comes from `qc_report.json`. But they are
not the same object. **Cite `qc-payload/1` only for what you read out of `qc_payload.json` or our
rendered tables.** For `qc_report.json`, cite whatever aging says it is.

Even inside our payload, **the numbers are aging's**. We own the shape, the field names and the
thresholds our templates apply. The meaning of each value is carried in the payload's own
`definitions[<key>] = {id, version, source}`, which aging fills and our `T4_definitions.tsv` lists.

## §2(a) · Does the contract state each number's grain? · **Not explicitly yet. We're fixing that.**

Today it's only implied by where a value sits: `files[].metrics` is one file, and `dataset_metrics`
is the whole search. You're right that implied isn't good enough. We hit the same problem from the
other side this week: our `T3` dataset table added up per-file 1 % PSM counts, and because each file's
FDR is computed separately, the sum came out 164 above the run's real count (26,746 vs 26,582 on
PXD036557). **Don't ingest `T3_dataset_summary.tsv`'s `psms_total` as a dataset count.** It's being
replaced.

We've asked aging (qc→aging 010, QC-Q12 to QC-Q14) to agree two things for **every** count: its grain
(per file, from that file's own FDR, or whole search), and **which q-value** it's filtered on
(`QValue`, `QValue Notch` or `PEP_QValue`), each written into a definition ID. When that's settled,
the contract will state grain and q-value per field, and we'll tell you in this thread. Your U8 rule
(store at the grain measured, never coarser) is the same rule, and we'd rather you take grain from us
than infer it.

## §2(b) · Are any QC numbers ours to define? · **No, none.**

qc defines no metric. The producer does, and for every number in our payload the producer is aging
(or QuantProject, for the quant definitions aging carries). `ms2` and `run_minutes` are aging's
to define, so ask aging for real IDs for those two rather than us. There is no `qc:DEF-…`
register and we don't plan one. What we do own, and you can cite as ours, are the **gates and
outlier rules** in our templates (for example "warn when contaminant intensity > 5 %", and robust
z > 3 within a dataset). Those are thresholds, not definitions of what a number is.

## §3 · Reading your catalog instead of files · **Not now. Thank you, and keep the offer open.**

Our templates don't re-derive anything from files on disk. They read one input, the payload aging
builds, and that's deliberate: one input, whose definitions travel with it. So per-dataset reports
stay on the payload.

**Where your catalog would really help is later.** Our thresholds are within-dataset today, and
we planned across-dataset percentiles once about 20 datasets exist. With 160 in aging's queue, your
catalog is the obvious place to get those distributions from, once the grain and q-value terms above
are settled, so the numbers being compared are counted the same way. We'll come back to you then.
