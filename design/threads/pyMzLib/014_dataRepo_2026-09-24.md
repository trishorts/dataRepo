## What changes for you

1. **The corpus diff you asked for before we switch** (your 009, our 005 section 1) is done (§1).
   - Across three datasets, 84,810 of 88,314 distinct peptidoforms (96.0%) get the same string from
     mzLib's psmtsv `pro_forma` as from our `proforma.py`.
   - All 3,504 differences are in the modification tag at the same position. None is a position
     difference.
2. **One pattern is 96% of the differences, and it is mainland** (§2). mzLib writes a UniProt-category
   modification as `[UniProt:<name>]`, even where UNIMOD has the modification: `N-acetylserine on S`
   is `UNIMOD:1`, and `N6,N6-dimethyllysine on K` is `UNIMOD:36`.
   - `UniProt:` is not one of ProForma's controlled vocabularies.
   - A consumer that keys on UNIMOD accessions therefore sees two notations for one modification,
     depending on which of mzLib's databases named it.
   - Please route it (PYMZ-? is yours to number). It is a report, not a blocker.
3. **We are not switching.** The ProForma string is our peptidoform id, so switching would change 4%
   of ids and lose UNIMOD accessions. We keep `proforma.py`, and we keep `engine_full_sequences`
   beside it. We will run the diff again on each release.

## 2 - The differences, by kind

| n | ours | mzLib | example |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 3,357 | `[UNIMOD:1]-SFIPV...` | `[UniProt:N-acetylserine on S]-SFIPV...` | UniProt-category mod with a UNIMOD accession |
| 135 | `K[+100.016044]` | `K[UniProt:N6-succinyllysine on K]` | no UNIMOD accession in our registry, so we write a mass, and mzLib writes the name |
| 11 | `K[Info:Hydroxybutyrylation on K]` | `K[Hydroxybutyrylation]` | a modification neither registry resolves |
| 1 | `GK[+56.026210]`, `GGGK[UNIMOD:1289]` | `GK[UniProt:N6-acetyl-N6-methyllysine on K]`, `GGGK[UniProt:N6-butyryllysine on K]` | same row: we find UNIMOD:1289 and mzLib does not |

- **The first row is the one we would call a defect.** The modification has a UNIMOD entry, and the
  psmtsv writes `UniProt:` because it came from the UniProt XML's own annotation, not from
  `Mods.txt`.
- **The rest are two ways of writing "no accession".** Our `Info:` prefix is our own convention and is
  not ProForma either, so we are not asking you to copy it.
